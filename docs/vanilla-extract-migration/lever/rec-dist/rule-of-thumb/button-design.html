Button Design

Make a button show what it does and what it's doing.

Style every state, say why it's off, and guard anything destructive.

IDLEHOVERPRESSLOADDONE
SILENT→TACTILE

Answer every press

Give each state and refusal a visible change, so no press feels lost.

Give every state a look

Give hover, press and focus a look. If nothing changes, a click feels like it missed and a keyboard user can't see where they are. Hover both, press both, then Tab to them.

Try it
Quiz

Mia uses only the keyboard. She presses Tab to reach Save. Pick the Save that shows her where she is.

Say why it's off

A disabled button that only fades leaves people guessing, most of all when the form looks done. Show the no-entry cursor, keep the label readable, and put the reason in a tooltip. Find out why Publish is off in both, then fix it.

Try it
Quiz

Can't be pressed

Publish stays off because an older post already uses this URL. Nothing else on screen says so. Pick how Publish looks when someone hovers it.

Tap the one that fits.

Keep a disabled button still on hover

A disabled button that lights up on hover says yes while its cursor says no, so people click and nothing happens. Keep its colour and its drop still on hover and press, and let the tooltip do the talking. Hover Back in both.

Fill one button per screen

Visual weight shows people the next step. Fill the main action in the brand colour, tint the supporting one, and leave Cancel as text. Keep red for destructive actions.

Try it

Guard the destructive action

Never let one stray click delete anything. Confirm with a red button that names what goes, ask people to hold the button down, or delete at once and offer Undo. Try all three.

Optical alignment over mathematical alignment

Take 4px off the icon's side. An icon has white space inside its own box, so equal padding makes that side look wider.

Try it

TL;DR

  1. Any buttonGive hover, press and focus a look, in 100 to 200ms
  2. Not available yetShow the no-entry cursor and a tooltip that says why
  3. Hovering a disabled buttonKeep its colour and drop still
  4. Picking a variantFill one per screen, tint the secondary, make Cancel text
  5. It deletes somethingMake it red, then confirm by name, hold, or offer Undo
  6. An icon beside the labelTake 4px off the icon's side
